Why test here

  • No sign-up, no install — measured in the browser, results immediately.
  • Average in milliseconds — several trials are averaged rather than judging you on one lucky tap.
  • Restraint is tested too — red no-go trials require you not to tap, which measures response inhibition.
  • Challenge a friend — share your result and see who is quicker.
  • Records kept — your best stays on your device so you can watch it improve.

How the test works

Tap as fast as you can the moment the screen turns green. Tapping before it changes counts as a false start.

Occasionally the screen turns red instead — a no-go trial, where the correct response is to hold still. After several rounds your average reaction time in milliseconds is reported.

You will be quicker when rested and focused, looking at the screen with your finger already hovering. Typical simple visual reaction time is around 200–270 ms; under 200 ms is genuinely fast.
