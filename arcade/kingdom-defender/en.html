Tower defenseAbout 8 min per round

A classic tower defense: build towers along the road to protect your kingdom from incoming waves.

How to play

Place towers on the build spots and upgrade them. Stop the enemies before they reach the castle.

Tips

  • Towers near a bend in the road get to fire for longer.
  • Upgrading towers in good spots usually beats building lots of weak ones.

Good for

Players who enjoy building a solid line of defense step by step
