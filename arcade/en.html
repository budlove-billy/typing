8 light web games picked by Mallow

After a few brain games it can be nice to let your mind rest. This page collects games that are good for exactly that. No install, no sign-up — just press play.

We picked them with three rules: a round takes 3–10 minutes, you can play from the pictures without reading, and the content is fine for families. FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, every game is free to play. Some show ads inside the game or offer optional items to buy.

Are my scores saved?

These games open from an external platform, so they are separate from your Mallow best scores, daily three and streak. Some games keep their own progress in your browser.

Is it OK for kids?

We only chose puzzle and casual games without violence or horror. Because there may be in-game ads and purchase screens, we recommend kids play with a parent.

The game won't open.

The first load can take around 10 seconds on a slow connection. If it still fails, press 'Try again' or pick another game. Games may also be removed or changed.
