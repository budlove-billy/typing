Take a Break

9 light web games picked by Mallow

After a few brain games it can be nice to let your mind rest. This page collects games that are good for exactly that. No install, no sign-up — just press play.

We picked them with three rules: a round takes 3–10 minutes, you can play from the pictures without reading, and the content is fine for families. No violent or scary games and nothing that looks like gambling. Each one is well rated by players, and we opened every game on a phone before adding it.

ⓘ These games are not made by Mallow; they are provided by the external game platform Playgama. They may show ads or offer optional purchases. Games played here do not count toward your Mallow records, missions or streaks.

Fruit Merge

Merge · physics puzzleAbout 5 min per round

The drop-and-merge style that went viral as the "watermelon game". Drop fruit into the box; when two of the same fruit touch, they become the next bigger fruit. The round ends when the pile crosses the line.

How to play

Move left or right, then release to drop the fruit.

Tips

  • Keep big fruit in one corner and grow smaller ones next to it.
  • Check the next fruit and leave a spot for it — that is how chains happen.

Good for

A short, mindless break with a little spatial thinking

Bubble Blast

Bubble shooterAbout 5 min per round

A long-loved bubble shooter. Shoot from the bottom and connect three or more of the same colour to pop them. Reach each level's goal to move on.

How to play

Aim with your finger and release to shoot. You can bounce shots off the walls.

Tips

  • Pop the bubbles holding up a cluster and everything below drops at once.
  • No match for your current bubble? Bank it off a wall onto a matching colour.

Good for

Anyone who wants a familiar, easy-going classic

Mahjong Lines

Mahjong connectAbout 7 min per round

Mahjong connect (also known as Shisen-sho). Two identical tiles disappear when you can join them with a line that does not cross other tiles.

How to play

Tap two matching tiles; if a path exists, both vanish.

Tips

  • Clear the edges first to open paths into the middle.
  • When four copies remain, remove the pair most likely to get blocked first.

Good for

Fans of classic tile matching who like calm observation

Find the Frog

Hidden objectsAbout 6 min per round

A hidden-object game where frogs are tucked away all over each picture. Every scene has a different theme, so there is always a new corner to explore. No reading needed.

How to play

Look around the picture and tap a frog when you find one.

Tips

  • Split the screen into four parts and sweep one at a time.
  • Look first where the background is a similar green — grass and leaves.

Good for

Observant players, or parents playing together with kids

Good Sort Master

Triple match · sortingAbout 5 min per round

A tidy-up puzzle: gather three of the same item on one shelf and they vanish. Items hidden behind come forward, so the order you clear things in matters.

How to play

Drag items to another slot. Three identical items together disappear.

Tips

  • Peek at the back row first and clear the shelf you need to open.
  • Save an empty slot as temporary storage for when you get stuck.

Good for

People who enjoy tidying up and thinking a few moves ahead

Vega Mix 2

Match-3About 6 min per round

A match-3 puzzle: swap two neighbouring pieces to line up three or more of the same. An adventure on a mysterious island gives you a reason to clear the next level.

How to play

Slide a piece sideways or up/down to swap it with its neighbour.

Tips

  • Matching four or five creates special pieces — don't rush plain threes.
  • Matches near the bottom make pieces fall from above and often trigger chains.

Good for

Match-3 fans who like steady progress

Wood Blocks Jam

Block escape puzzleAbout 5 min per round

Slide coloured blocks out through the gate of the same colour. Blocks get in each other's way, so the puzzle is working out which one goes first.

How to play

Drag a block to slide it. When it reaches its matching gate, it leaves the board.

Tips

  • Start with the big block that blocks the most paths.
  • Stuck? Think backwards from the last few moves to find the open lane.

Good for

Logic puzzle fans who like to think one move at a time

Favorite Puzzles

JigsawAbout 10 min per round

Jigsaw puzzles with a variety of pictures. You choose from 6 up to 1,200 pieces, so it works for kids and adults alike.

How to play

Drag a piece to its spot and it snaps in place. Pick the piece count at the start.

Tips

  • Build the border first.
  • Leave big single-colour areas like sky or water for last and match them by shape.

Good for

A slow, calm focus session — choose fewer pieces on a small phone

Prison Escape Parkour

Parkour · escape runAbout 8 min per round

A 3D parkour game where you run and jump through an obstacle course to break out of prison. Dodge the traps, cross the platforms and clear each section until you reach the exit. A popular game with lots of player reviews.

How to play

On a phone, use the on-screen move and jump buttons; on a computer, use the arrow keys (WASD) and Space.

Tips

  • Look at the distance to the next platform before jumping, and keep jumps short.
  • If you fall you restart from a section you already passed, so take it one section at a time.

Good for

Anyone who wants a hands-on change of pace, or likes running and jumping games

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, every game is free to play. Some show ads inside the game or offer optional items to buy.

Are my scores saved?

These games open from an external platform, so they are separate from your Mallow best scores, daily three and streak. Some games keep their own progress in your browser.

Is it OK for kids?

We only chose puzzle and casual games without violence or horror. Because there may be in-game ads and purchase screens, we recommend kids play with a parent.

The game won't open.

The first load can take around 10 seconds on a slow connection. If it still fails, press 'Try again' or pick another game. Games may also be removed or changed.
